About Brain Tumors

A brain tumor is an abnormal growth of cells inside the brain. Tumors can affect brain function by increasing pressure inside the skull or damaging nearby tissues.

🔴 Malignant Tumors (Cancerous)

Malignant tumors are aggressive brain tumors that can grow rapidly and invade nearby brain tissues.

  • Grow rapidly
  • Aggressive in nature
  • Can invade nearby tissues
  • Life-threatening if untreated

Common Malignant Tumor Types

  • Glioblastoma (GBM) — Highly aggressive and fast-growing brain cancer.
  • Anaplastic Astrocytoma — Malignant tumor originating from astrocyte cells.
  • Medulloblastoma — Rapidly growing tumor commonly seen in children.
  • High-Grade Glioma — Severe form of glioma with aggressive progression.

🟢 Benign Tumors (Non-Cancerous)

Benign tumors usually grow slowly and are less likely to spread into surrounding tissues.

  • Grow slowly
  • Less aggressive
  • Usually localized
  • Lower risk of spreading

Common Benign Tumor Types

  • Meningioma — Usually slow-growing tumor arising from the meninges.
  • Pituitary Adenoma — Tumor affecting the pituitary gland and hormone balance.
  • Schwannoma — Tumor developing from nerve sheath cells.
  • Low-Grade Glioma — Slower-growing glioma with lower aggressiveness.

⚠️ Common Symptoms

  • Persistent headaches
  • Blurred vision
  • Seizures
  • Memory problems
  • Difficulty speaking
  • Balance issues
  • Vomiting or nausea

⚙️ AI Processing Pipeline

Model 1

Brain MRI Validation

Checks whether the uploaded image is a valid brain MRI scan.

  • Model: ResNet50 CNN
  • Task: Brain vs Non-Brain
  • Prevents invalid inputs

Model 2

Tumor Classification

Determines whether the MRI contains tumor and classifies tumor type.

  • Model: ResNet50 CNN
  • Glioma
  • Meningioma
  • Pituitary
  • No Tumor

Model 3

Tumor Segmentation

Highlights tumor regions inside MRI scans.

  • Model: UNet Segmentation
  • Segmentation Mask
  • Tumor Localization
  • Better interpretability
